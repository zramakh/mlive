## Checks

* **Data:** the last 11 Power Toto draws (23 Sep – 17 Oct 2026) match lottolyzer.com exactly (draws 6189–6199), so
  number 28 really came up 6 times in the last 10 draws (26 Sep, 30 Sep, 3, 10, 11 and 14 Oct).
* **No copy errors behind the repeats:** consecutive draws sharing 3 or more numbers occur at the rate expected for fair
  draws (6/50 25 vs 25.2, 6/55 47 vs 38.1, 6/58 27 vs 31.8, Life 306 vs 317.0); 4 or more: 4 vs 1.3, 1 vs 1.8, 2 vs 1.4,
  69 vs 72.3. The few close pairs are genuine, different draws.
* **Window-to-window persistence of repeats** is positive in all four games (+0.14, +0.08, +0.10, +0.03; fair ≈ 0).
  Combined over the four games (Fisher) p ≈ 0.01, so a weak short-term clustering of repeats may be real. It is too
  weak to use: playing "repeat the previous draw" only after a high-repeat window returned 1.00×, 1.04×, 1.05× and
  0.79× of chance, all inside the fair 5–95% range.

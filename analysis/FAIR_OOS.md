# A fair unseen-draw test for every lead

Generated by `analysis/fair_oos.py`. Before each year, every lead's record on the earlier years only is turned into a z against chance (drawn numbers in its top ticket: 8 Magnum Life, 6 Toto); the top 1% of leads of the game are "chosen" and that year is their blind test. Pooled over every year after the first two, so each game gets thousands of unseen lead-draws. Shifted = parameter values rolled against the draws (no link to the sky).

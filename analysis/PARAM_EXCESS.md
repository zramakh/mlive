# Which astrological parameters carry the real calendar's extra high hits

Generated by `analysis/param_excess.py`. *Excess* of a lead = its high hits on the real calendar minus its mean on 20 shifted calendars (earlier draws, all four formats added; 6+ numbers for Magnum Life, 5+ for Toto). A parameter's score = average excess of the leads using it (alone or paired; 230 leads each). *z* = that score against the same score computed with each shifted calendar treated as real. With 114 parameters, a few |z| > 2.5 are expected by chance.
